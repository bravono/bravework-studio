# Four-Part Product Funnel & Pricing Framework

This reference outlines the structural tiers, duration formulas, and commercial pricing models enforced by the Course Architect Agent.

## Product Funnel Architecture

### 1. 2-Hour Live Teaser (Front-End Acquisition)
- **Primary Goal**: Conversion from free or low-barrier audience to paid tier enrollments.
- **Duration**: Exactly 120 minutes (2 hours).
- **Structure**:
  - `00:00 - 00:15` (15 min): Rapid Onboarding, tool setup verification, and quick-win introduction.
  - `00:15 - 00:50` (35 min): Core Concept Demonstration, foundational mental models, live workflow run.
  - `00:50 - 01:40` (50 min): Interactive Hands-On Exercise, guided build with verification checkpoint.
  - `01:40 - 02:00` (20 min): Tier Upsell Roadmap, curriculum comparison, and Q&A.
- **Pricing Strategy**: Free webinar, or nominal ticket price ($10 to $29) credited towards any tier purchase.

### 2. Tier 1: Beginner (Foundations & Tooling)
- **Primary Goal**: Rapid operational literacy, eliminating tool friction, self-contained project completion.
- **Duration**: 8 to 15 instructional hours (typically 4 to 6 modules).
- **Curriculum Scope**: Interface navigation, keybindings, primitive manipulation, file structure, core mechanics.
- **Capstone Project**: Self-contained functional technical asset (e.g. foundational robotic actuator, basic mechanical bracket, parametric room enclosure).
- **Target Audience**: Beginners with zero or minimal prior domain exposure.

### 3. Tier 2: Intermediate (Complex Workflows & Optimization)
- **Primary Goal**: Production speed, pipeline standards, procedural patterns, and real-world asset delivery.
- **Duration**: 16 to 30 instructional hours (typically 6 to 10 modules).
- **Curriculum Scope**: Sub-division surface modeling, procedural rigging, non-destructive modifiers, UV unwrapping, material shaders, performance budgeting.
- **Capstone Project**: Production-ready sub-assembly (e.g. 6-axis robotic arm with inverse kinematics, multi-story architectural pavilion with lighting analysis).
- **Target Audience**: Practitioners wanting career-level velocity and portfolio standard assets.

### 4. Tier 3: Advanced (Industry-Grade Execution & Pipelines)
- **Primary Goal**: Mastery, edge case resolution, multi-tool interoperability, automation scripts.
- **Duration**: 24 to 45 instructional hours (typically 8 to 12 modules).
- **Curriculum Scope**: Custom Python scripting, automated batch baking, geometry node networks, high-to-low poly LOD baking, pipeline integration.
- **Capstone Project**: Commercial-grade end-to-end pipeline deliverable (e.g. fully automated parametric chassis pipeline, industrial digital twin simulation).
- **Target Audience**: Advanced professionals, studio technical artists, and lead engineers.
